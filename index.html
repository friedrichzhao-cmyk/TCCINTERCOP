Brief SOP 简要办理流程
1
Notification Letter of Foreigner's Work Permit《外国人工作许可通知》
Applied for while still abroad, using the checklist below; a physical exam at a designated hospital may be required.
Est. 7–10 working days
2
Z Work Visa (Overseas)Z字工作签证（境外办理）
At a Chinese Embassy/Consulate: valid passport, white-background photo, original Notification Letter. Visa sticker affixed once approved.
Est. 3–7 working days
3
Enter China with the Z Visa持Z签证入境中国
Travel to Tianjin and enter China on the approved Z Work Visa.
4
Residence Permit for Work入境后办理工作类居留许可
For stays over 90 days; completed after arrival, coordinated by TCC/YuanWang.
Note 说明： Time frames are the estimates stated in the procedure shared by YuanWang — not a commitment by DPU for your specific case. Additional documents may be requested.
Short-Term Checklist 短期 · 90天以内
Passport front page scan护照首页扫描件
Passport-size photo, white background, 40–140K白底护照尺寸照片，40–140K
Work permit application form工作许可申请表
Physical exam report体检报告
Stamp on photo (p.1); sign + stamp on p.2; doctor's note must state "Health" or "Fit to work".
Email address & phone number电子邮箱及电话号码
Long-Term Checklist 长期 · 超过90天
Passport front page scan + white-background photo (40–140K)护照首页扫描件 + 白底照片
Original physical exam report体检报告原件
Same stamping requirements as short-term ("Health" / "Fit to work" on p.2).
Reference letter proving 2 years' work experience证明2年工作经验的推荐信
Show working period, position title, company address, contact if available.
BA/MA degree certificate — original colour scan + legalization (Apostille) colour scan学位证书原件彩色扫描件 + 认证（Apostille）彩色扫描件
Non-criminal record certificate — original colour scan + legalization colour scan无犯罪记录证明原件彩色扫描件 + 认证彩色扫描件
Must be notarized and legalized at a Chinese embassy.
